# What This Proves And Does Not Prove

## This Can Prove

- The record has the required minimal AEP structure.
- The demo signature matches the current bundle content.
- Key actions that require receipts have matching receipt references.
- High-risk actions have or lack approved human review.
- Contract review conclusions point back to source clause references or source locations.
- Selected referenced content has not changed when `content_hashes.target_path` matches the actual value.
- The bundle avoids known forbidden raw-data and secret field names.

## This Does Not Prove

- The model conclusion is correct.
- External-world facts are true.
- The system is fully compliant.
- Legal responsibility is automatically removed.
- The demo signature is a production legal signature.
- The platform is a complete agent governance SaaS.
